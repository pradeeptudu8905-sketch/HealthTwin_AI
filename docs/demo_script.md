# Suggested 20-minute demo video plan

Keep the recording at least 20 minutes, as required by the competition page. Show the actual application running and explain the limitations honestly.

1. **0:00–2:00 — Introduction:** Team, project title, healthcare problem and intended use.
2. **2:00–4:00 — Digital Twin concept:** Explain how a virtual patient profile is updated with sensor readings.
3. **4:00–7:00 — Data streams:** Show synthetic historical record fields and simulated wearable readings.
4. **7:00–10:00 — Architecture:** Explain data preparation, feature assembly, model and dashboard.
5. **10:00–14:00 — Live demo:** Change glucose, sleep, heart rate and history inputs; show the risk estimate and trend charts changing.
6. **14:00–16:00 — Model evaluation:** Explain the held-out synthetic test metric and why it does not establish clinical validity.
7. **16:00–18:00 — Privacy and safety:** Explain synthetic data, no real patient data, and limitations.
8. **18:00–20:00 — Outcomes and next steps:** Summarize what works, known limitations and future improvements.

Do not claim medical accuracy, real-time clinical deployment, or prevention of actual events.
